Plaid bank feed for Accounting (sandbox)

Connects Bank of America to the Accounting app through Plaid, so transactions
arrive on their own instead of being parsed out of monthly PDF statements.

Built natively in PHP rather than from Plaid's Node quickstart. VGo runs on
LiteSpeed shared hosting with no Node runtime and no way to keep a process
alive, so a Node service would have meant a second host, a second deploy path,
and the access token living outside the app that needs it. Everything Plaid
requires is JSON over HTTPS plus one ES256 signature check, and a probe on the
live server confirmed both work there before any of this was written.

Plaid gets no parallel transaction store. A synced transaction becomes an
ordinary acc_bank_imports / acc_bank_lines row, exactly like a line parsed from
a statement, so the matcher, Bank Review and the reconciliation engine treat
both identically and nothing downstream learned a second shape. Lines arrive as
`pending`; nothing reaches the ledger without a human accepting it.

Two guards exist because these three accounts already hold years of imported
history. Per-account `sync_from` skips anything on or before the cutover, and a
content-level check catches the overlap that identity dedupe cannot see —
Plaid's transaction_id can never match a fitid minted by the PDF importer, so
without it the same charge would land twice.

Plaid reports money LEAVING an account as positive; acc_bank_lines is signed
from the account's point of view. Every amount is negated on the way in. Getting
that backwards would post every payment as income, so it has a test.

Credentials live in config/plaid.local.php — above the docroot, gitignored, and
written by an in-app settings panel so keys never pass through a chat or a
commit. The access_token is sealed at rest, so a database dump alone is not
enough to read the company's bank feed.

The webhook is the only unauthenticated route in the router. It verifies an
ES256 JWT against Plaid's published key, rejects anything older than five
minutes, and compares a SHA-256 of the RAW body before trusting a single field.
It handles the Bank of America migration explicitly: PENDING_DISCONNECT gives
about a week to re-link through Link update mode, and migrated Items carry a
12-month consent expiry, so both raise a notification that deep-links to
Banking rather than waiting for someone to notice a feed gone quiet.

Tested offline: 22 assertions covering token sealing (including that the wrong
key cannot open a token), the JWK->PEM and raw->DER conversions that make
signature verification work, tampered-signature rejection, the sign convention
both directions, and six overlap-guard cases against realistic statement text.
UI render-checked at 390px and 1280px for overflow and the re-auth state.

Sandbox only — env is 'sandbox' and no production secret is stored.
